Methodology

Shipped counts work a maintainer accepted through a merge, cherry-pick, release reference, or another PR that credits the original contribution.

Withdrawn means the author pulled the work. Superseded means a maintainer replaced it. Lost means competing work prevailed. PRs appear newest first, using close time for closed PRs and creation time for open PRs.

Leaderboards rank outside contributors, excluding owners, employees, collaborators with write access, and bots. Each board shows up to five contributors and expands to the top 20. Contributor lists combine recent activity, cached records, and hermes-webui's CONTRIBUTORS.md.

hermes-webui counts distinct PRs credited in release notes, eligible merges, and coauthored release commits. Other repos use acceptance evidence for their top 10 contributors and merge counts below that.
