FTE Applications During SSN Renewal

Why applying to W-2 roles while your SSN is processing is not disingenuous, and how to handle the conversation.

The Situation

You're a legal permanent resident. Your SSN renewal will take 2+ months due to the full DHS background check for LPRs. You're applying to roles posted as FTE on LinkedIn. You'd prefer to work C2C through your LLC.

Why It's Not Disingenuous

LPR status is unconditional employment authorization. You are legally allowed to work in the United States, full stop. The SSN renewal is a bureaucratic delay that affects W-2 payroll setup, not your right to work.

I-9 verification (which every employer must complete) doesn't even require an SSN. Employers can use other List A, B, or C documents to verify identity and work authorization. The SSN is needed for payroll tax reporting, which is a W-2 onboarding detail, not a hiring eligibility question.

What you're dealing with is a timing gap between "legally allowed to work" and "can be set up on a W-2 payroll." That's a logistics problem, not an honesty problem.

How Startups Handle This

This is common. The standard solution is: start as 1099/C2C, convert to W-2 when the paperwork clears. Some companies will do it without blinking; others won't have the flexibility. But that's their constraint to evaluate, not yours to preemptively screen yourself out over.

Early-stage startups (~30 people) are often more flexible on this than large companies, especially if you frame it as simpler for them: no benefits overhead, no payroll tax burden during the interim period.

When and How to Bring It Up

Recruiter screen

Don't volunteer it. This call is about mutual fit: whether the role excites you and whether you excite them. Employment structure is a negotiation detail for after they've decided they want you.

If asked directly about work authorization

"Yes, I'm a permanent resident. Fully authorized to work in the US, no sponsorship needed."

That's the complete, honest answer. The SSN renewal doesn't change any of it.

At the offer stage

This is the right moment. Once they want you, you have leverage, and they're solving a logistics problem rather than making a go/no-go decision:

"I'm a permanent resident, fully work-authorized, but my SSN renewal is processing. I can start immediately as C2C through my LLC and convert to W-2 once it clears, probably [timeframe]. Does that work for you?"

What not to do

Don't self-select out of FTE postings. If you sit out 2+ months of pipeline for a reason most hiring managers would consider a non-issue, you're losing momentum during a period where you're actively building interview skills and market presence.

The Path: Green Card and SSN Replacement

Both cards lost (same wallet). Foreign passport expired, no US passport. This creates an I-9 verification gap for W-2 employment. C2C through Inkreel (EIN letter on hand) sidesteps it entirely.

Step 1: File I-90 (Replace Green Card) with Fee Waiver

  • Form I-90: uscis.gov/i-90
  • Form I-912 (fee waiver): uscis.gov/i-912
  • Filing fee is $465 (paper only when requesting fee waiver; online filing doesn't support I-912).
  • Qualify for full waiver on two independent grounds: (1) active means-tested benefits (Cash Assistance, SNAP, Medicaid), (2) income at $0, well under 150% FPL (~$22,590 for one person).
  • Attach HRA Statement of Benefits (FIA-1034b) or budget letter showing active status. The Statement of Benefits from ACCESS HRA works; it shows the agency, benefit types, active status, and payment history.
  • Mail both forms together to the address in the I-90 instructions for New York.

Step 2: Request ADIT Stamp (Temporary I-551)

  • Once the I-797C receipt arrives, call USCIS at 1-800-375-5283 and request the ADIT stamp via mail delivery.
  • Since the foreign passport is expired, USCIS places the stamp on a Form I-94 (printed with photo from USCIS records). This is the standard output for the mail process regardless of passport status.
  • The I-94 with ADIT stamp is legally equivalent to a stamp in a passport. SSA POMS RM 10211.025 (updated January 2025) explicitly accepts it.
  • Typical turnaround: days to a few weeks after requesting. Mention employment urgency on the call to expedite.

Step 3: SSA Appointment

  • July 15, 2026, 2:00 PM
  • Bring: I-94 with ADIT stamp + state ID or other identity document.
  • SSA will run a SAVE verification against USCIS records to confirm LPR status.
  • Known risk: field office staff sometimes don't recognize the I-94 format. If challenged, ask for a supervisor and reference POMS RM 10211.025. Carry a printout of that POMS section and the USCIS mail-delivery announcement.

What the ADIT Stamp Covers

  • SSA: Proof of LPR status for SSN card replacement (POMS RM 10211.025).
  • I-9: List A document (covers both identity and employment authorization). Valid until stamp expiration or 1 year from issuance.
  • Timeline: File I-90 now → receipt in ~2-3 weeks → call for ADIT stamp → stamped I-94 arrives before July 15 SSA appointment → SSN card mailed ~2 weeks after SSA visit.
