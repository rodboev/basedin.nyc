Interview Prep: Revin

AI Systems Engineer · $200K–$275K + equity · NYC on-site · Recruiter screen June 11, 2026

Sections

What Revin Does

Revin (revin.ai) builds AI agents for home-services call centers. Their platform handles SMS, voice, and email conversations on behalf of large residential service operators: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, home remodeling. The agents book appointments, follow up on unsold estimates, manage memberships, handle after-hours calls, and rescue missed leads.

The integration story is the moat. Revin plugs directly into field service management and CRM systems like ServiceTitan, Salesforce, LeadPerfection, i360, and Acculynx. When a $100M+ operator misses a call or drops a lead, that's real revenue lost. Revin's AI answers 24/7, qualifies intent, and books directly into the operator's system of record.

Results they claim

  • $470K+ recovered from unsold estimates in 3 months
  • 10% higher booking rate than average human CSRs
  • 32% of all booked appointments set by AI
  • 500+ appointments booked in two weeks for one customer
  • Near-zero second hold times during peak periods
  • 24/7/365 coverage with no staffing overhead

Customers

Sears Home Services, Del Air, Long Home Remodeling, ImproveIt Remodeling, Sage Home, Lion Home Service, PipeDreams, and others. These are large, multi-location operators.

Delivery model

Forward-deployed, white-glove. Each customer gets a dedicated Revin engineer who reviews their scripts and SOPs line-by-line, integrates into their CRM/FSM, and does onsite activation. This is a Palantir-style delivery model applied to home services, not an off-the-shelf chatbot.

Stage and Funding

  • Founded: 2022 (originally called "Authentic," pivoted to current model mid-2023)
  • HQ: 900 Broadway, Suite 1003, New York, NY 10003 (Flatiron)
  • Total raised: ~$14.5M. Seed in 2022 (~$1.9M), then $10.7M in late 2025
  • Team: Small, early-stage. Hiring aggressively across engineering
  • Growth signal: LinkedIn followers up 257% YoY
  • Market: Home services is massive, fragmented, and tech-underserved. AI agents for call centers are one of the most proven LLM use cases: structured workflows, clear success metrics, measurable ROI

The Role: AI Systems Engineer

You'd build the production systems behind Revin's AI agents. When an agent makes a mistake (books outside hours, dispatches to the wrong zip code, promises a service the business doesn't offer) it costs the customer real money. Your job is to build the systems that prevent that.

Day-to-day

  • Build and evolve LangGraph workflows that handle SMS and voice conversations for enterprise operators
  • Ship features end-to-end across a Python/FastAPI backend and a Next.js/TypeScript dashboard
  • Work in a multi-tenant Supabase/Postgres environment with RLS and JWT-based access
  • Optimize omni-channel delivery across SMS, voice, and email
  • Build evaluation frameworks for AI agents to improve accuracy and reduce errors
  • Partner directly with product, design, and leadership

Tech stack

  • Backend: Python, FastAPI, LangGraph
  • Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript
  • Data: Supabase/Postgres, RLS, JWT-based access
  • Infra: Cloud-based, event-driven, REST APIs
  • Voice: LiveKit + OpenAI Realtime
  • Comms: SMS, voice, email
  • AI: OpenAI, LangGraph/LangChain
  • Observability: Langfuse, LangSmith, or Helicone (bonus)

What they want

  • 4–8 years fullstack experience shipping production software at a startup, high-velocity SaaS, or AI-driven company
  • Production LLM experience: shipped LLM-powered features that real customers depend on, not prototypes
  • Fluent in Python (FastAPI) and TypeScript (Next.js, React Query)
  • Comfortable with multi-tenant production systems: RLS, account-scoped queries, JWT-based access
  • Experience debugging APIs, tracing distributed issues, event-driven systems, unstructured data pipelines
  • Customer-obsessed, comfortable with ambiguity
  • Bonus: LangGraph/LangChain in production, real-time voice/audio (LiveKit, Daily.co, Twilio Voice), LLM observability tooling

Why You're a Strong Fit

1. You know the domain

Years at Liberty Pest Control mean firsthand experience with scheduling, dispatching, missed calls, customer follow-up, and the chaos of running a trades operation. That's rare in an engineering candidate, and it's exactly the context Revin's about page says they built the company around. Speak both languages: engineering and operations.

2. You've built AI agents in production

Your Notes AI project was built on GPT-4o and OpenAI's Realtime Voice API, with streaming Chat Completions for text and WebSocket relay for live AI-driven phone calls. Revin's listing confirms their voice stack is LiveKit + OpenAI Realtime, the same API you've shipped in production. Your pr-sweep orchestration (multi-agent workflows across Claude and Codex) adds the multi-model dimension. You've dealt with the hard parts: routing different tasks to different models, handling failures and retries, maintaining quality under real-world conditions.

3. The stack is your stack

Python, React, TypeScript, Supabase/Postgres, REST APIs, LLM integrations. No ramp-up on a new ecosystem; this is applying existing experience at higher stakes.

4. Multi-model orchestration experience

pr-sweep routes planning to models with strong architectural judgment, fans out implementation to faster models in parallel, and sends code review to a different provider entirely. This is exactly the kind of systems thinking Revin needs: knowing which model to use for which task, and building the infrastructure to make that work reliably.

5. Open source credibility

85 shipped PRs across hermes-webui, hermes-agent, and claude-mem (#4 on hermes-webui with 70 credited). Security hardening, performance work, new features. All reviewed and accepted by external maintainers, not internal PRs.

Talking Points to Prepare

The Liberty Pest Control angle

Don't be shy about this. Revin's entire pitch is "we built this out of the field, for the field." Their about page says they spent time inside call centers, dispatch rooms, and ops teams. You actually ran tech for a service business. Talk about the pain points you saw firsthand: missed calls turning into lost jobs, the scheduling chaos, the gap between what CRM tools promise and what actually happens in a dispatch room. Then connect it to why you're excited about what Revin is solving.

AI agents, not chatbots

Revin is careful to distinguish themselves from off-the-shelf chatbots. When talking about your AI work, emphasize the systems engineering: evaluation frameworks, error prevention, multi-model routing, handling edge cases. They want someone who understands that the hard part isn't getting an LLM to generate text; it's preventing it from booking a plumber at 3 AM when the business closes at 6 PM.

The gap year

Frame it the way you've rehearsed: open source contributions, AI integration work, multi-model orchestration. But add a new beat here: "I've been building AI systems that automate real workflows, and I want to do that at a company where the stakes are real revenue for real businesses." That threads directly into Revin's mission.

Things to Watch For

  • On-site 5 days/week. This is non-negotiable per the listing. Make sure you're comfortable with that before going deeper.
  • Small team, big customers. Sears Home Services is a large enterprise. Ask how they manage the support load with a small team. Burnout risk is real.
  • Forward-deployed model blurring lines. The Palantir comparison cuts both ways. Ask whether you'd be building platform or doing bespoke customer work. The listing says platform, but in practice early-stage companies blur this.
  • Pivot history. The Authentic-to-Revin pivot is fine (good, even), but worth understanding: how much of the current codebase was built from scratch vs. carried over? How settled is the product direction?
  • "Top-tier education" language. This appeared on Revin's Fullstack Engineer listing, not on this one. The AI Systems Engineer posting doesn't mention education. Not a concern here.
